How I work

I’m a product designer working across SaaS products, internal tools and user-facing platforms. I started in illustration and graphic design, and that eye for detail carried into UI/UX.

Think in systems

I turn a business problem into a workflow that makes sense to the person using it, and keep the interface consistent as the product grows. I care as much about the token architecture behind a button as how it feels to click.

Use AI intentionally

AI is part of my daily workflow: Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Figma Make and Replit help me explore concepts, prototype, write documentation and refine UX copy. It lets me iterate faster and validate earlier, so I can spend more time on real user problems.

Collaborate early

I work with engineers and PMs throughout, not just at handoff, because the decisions that matter happen when you’re working out what’s technically realistic and what users need. I use tools like Cursor to prototype interactions and communicate design intent.
